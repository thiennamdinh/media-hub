# Example media-hub agent questions

1. What are the most important stories from the last 24 hours, grouped by topic and source type?
2. Which links are showing up across multiple sources — e.g. RSS + Bluesky + Mastodon — and who is amplifying them?
3. What cybersecurity-related items appeared in the last week, ranked by novelty or cross-source attention?
4. Which Polymarket probabilities moved the most since yesterday, and do any map to news items in the hub?
5. What are Bluesky/Mastodon security / AI / geopolitics people talking about today that hasn’t hit mainstream RSS yet?
6. Show me stories where social discussion is high but traditional news coverage is low.
7. What sources have been noisy or low-value lately, and which should I remove or downweight?
8. What topics are under-covered by my current configs relative to my interests? Suggest new RSS feeds, Bluesky actors/lists, Mastodon accounts/tags/instances, or Polymarket markets.
9. Find clusters of records that seem to describe the same underlying event, even if their URLs differ.
10. Give me a morning intelligence brief: top technical/security stories, geopolitical risk signals, market probability shifts, and anything unusually viral.
